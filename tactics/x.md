## Recognizing it

`x.com` / `twitter.com` URLs; lane briefs naming accounts, lists, or "what are
practitioners saying about X".

## Finding content (search & discovery)

- Anonymous web access is heavily gated; timelines and search generally
  require a session. Prefer indirect routes first: web-search
  `site:x.com <topic>` for individually-indexed posts, and the person's blog/
  newsletter where threads usually get expanded anyway
  (unverified seed, 2026-07-10).
- Native search operators (with a session): `from:user`, `min_faves:N`,
  `filter:links`, `since:`/`until:` — `min_faves` is the fastest noise filter
  (unverified seed, 2026-07-10).
- Curated lists beat follower graphs for finding who's substantive on a topic
  (unverified seed, 2026-07-10).

## Judging quality fast

- A thread that cites artifacts (repos, papers, dashboards, code) outranks
  hot takes at any engagement level; check what a viral claim links to before
  writing a finding from it (unverified seed, 2026-07-10).

## Capture notes

- Posts are ephemeral and delete-prone: for anything evidence-grade, prefer a
  prompt one-off capture (`scope=page`) over a reference link
  (unverified seed, 2026-07-10).
